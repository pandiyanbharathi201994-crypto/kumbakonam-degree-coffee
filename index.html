History of Kumbakonam Degree Coffee ☕

Kumbakonam is well known for its traditional South Indian filter coffee. The coffee is traditionally prepared using freshly brewed decoction and milk, creating a rich and aromatic taste.

Over the years, this traditional coffee has become a special part of Kumbakonam's food culture and continues to be enjoyed by coffee lovers.
